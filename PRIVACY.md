# Dream Privacy Policy

*Last updated September 2026*

Dream is a dream journal. It doesn't want your data, and it collects as little as it can.

## What stays on your device
Your work lives in your own browser or app storage. We never see it. Clear your browser data and it's gone.

## What leaves your device
- **AI.** Text you send for an answer goes to the AI provider that produces it. It isn't used to train anything by us.
- **Voice.** If you speak a dream, the microphone is used only while you record, and only with your permission.
- **Your account.** Sign-in and the things you post are stored in our database on Supabase. You can ask us to delete your account and everything in it. This only applies if you sign in.
- **Site analytics.** The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites.

## What we never do
No ads. No selling data. No tracking cookies.

## Kids
Dream isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
